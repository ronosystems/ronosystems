Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or switch to a different plan at any time from your dashboard. Changes take effect at your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial?

Absolutely. Every new account starts on a free trial — no credit card required. Upgrade whenever you're ready.

What payment methods do you accept?

M-Pesa (STK, Paybill, Buy Goods), bank transfer, and card payments. Manual payments can be verified by our team.

Do you offer custom plans?

Yes — for Enterprise customers we offer custom plans, SLAs, and dedicated support. Contact us to discuss.

What happens if I exceed my limits?

We'll notify you when you're nearing a limit. You can upgrade instantly — no downtime, no data loss.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your dashboard — your plan stays active until the end of your billing period.
