Everything You Need to Run Your Business

Powerful Tools, One Platform

{{ system_name|default:"RonoSystems" }} gives you everything you need to sell, manage, and grow — from inventory and payments to analytics and AI. No add-ons, no surprises, no limits on how far you can scale.

Inventory Management
Track stock across multiple warehouses, receive low-stock alerts, and manage variants, batches, and serial numbers with ease.
Real-time Analytics
Live dashboards with sales, profit, and performance metrics. Slice by product, location, employee, or time period.
AI-Powered Insights
Smart recommendations, demand forecasting, and anomaly detection that help you stock smarter and sell more.
Team Management
Roles, permissions, shifts, and payroll — all in one place. Give every employee exactly the access they need.
Invoicing & Billing
Generate professional invoices, track payments, automate reminders, and reconcile receipts against bank statements.
M-Pesa & Payments
Accept STK Push, Paybill, Buy Goods, bank transfers, and cards. Everything reconciles automatically into your books.
Enterprise Security
Bank-grade encryption, 2FA, audit logs, and role-based access — built to pass the strictest compliance reviews.
Cloud + Offline
Works on any device. Keeps selling when the internet drops, and syncs everything the moment you're back online.
Integrations
WhatsApp, email, Cloudinary, eTIMS, and a REST API to connect anything else you already run.
